# Week 10 - Day 5: Code Review, Feedback & Certification

## Overview

In this day, I reviewed the final evaluation structure of the 10-week BinX Backend Internship and prepared for the program's final code review, feedback, certification outcome, and wrap-up.

The final evaluation combines technical skill development, sprint and deliverable quality, professionalism and consistency, and the final presentation.

Because the final code review, official evaluation scores, written feedback, and certification result are provided by BinX Tech after the final assessment process, these sections are documented as preparation plans until the official results are received.

The day also included preparing a short personal reflection on the full 10-week internship experience and the engineering practices developed throughout the program.

---

## Evaluation Weights

The final program grade is based on four weighted evaluation criteria:

| Criteria | Weight | Assessed By |
|---|---:|---|
| Technical Skill Development | 40% | Mentor |
| Sprint / Deliverable Quality | 30% | Mentor + Review Panel |
| Professionalism & Consistency | 20% | Mentor |
| Final Presentation | 10% | Review Panel |

The final presentation represents only one part of the overall evaluation.

Most of the final grade reflects the technical progress, deliverable quality, professionalism, and consistency demonstrated throughout the full 10-week program.

---

## Final Code Review Preparation

A final code review will be completed with a senior BinX Tech engineer after the team project reaches its finished state.

The review will focus on the complete capstone codebase, including:

- Overall architecture and project structure.
- Code quality and maintainability.
- Automated test coverage.
- Integration between ASP.NET Core and FastAPI.
- Error handling and response validation.
- Security practices.
- Important engineering decisions and their technical reasoning.
- Areas that could be improved in future iterations.

The review will cover the complete project rather than a single sprint or isolated feature.

Any feedback from the review will be documented and used in the final program wrap-up.

## Passing Grade & Program Outcomes

A minimum final grade of **70/100** is required to pass the program and receive the BinX Tech Certificate of Completion.

The official program outcomes are:

| Final Grade | Outcome |
|---|---|
| 85–100 | Outstanding |
| 70–84 | Pass |
| 50–69 | Conditional Pass |
| Below 50 | Does Not Pass |

### Outstanding — 85 to 100

The outcome includes:

- Certificate of Completion.
- Letter of Recommendation.
- Priority consideration for employment opportunities at BinX Tech.

### Pass — 70 to 84

The outcome includes:

- Certificate of Completion.

### Conditional Pass — 50 to 69

A structured improvement plan is issued by the mentor.

The certificate is awarded after all required gaps are formally closed.

### Does Not Pass — Below 50

No certificate is issued.

Detailed written feedback is provided, and the case is reviewed by the program coordinator to determine the appropriate next step.

## Personal Reflection

The 10-week BinX Backend Internship helped me strengthen both my technical knowledge and my practical development workflow.

Throughout the program, I worked with C#, ASP.NET Core, Entity Framework Core, SQL Server, authentication and authorization, testing, performance optimization, deployment, and team-based project development.

One of the most valuable parts of the internship was learning how to make technical decisions based on evidence instead of assumptions.

I also improved my ability to test features before considering them complete, review code more carefully, document my work clearly, and explain technical decisions during discussions and presentations.

The program also helped me understand the importance of consistency, teamwork, code quality, testing, security, and continuous improvement.

The internship may end after Week 10, but the engineering habits developed during the program will continue to guide how I approach future backend projects and real development work.
